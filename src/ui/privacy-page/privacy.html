Privacy in the FuzeVPN extension

This notice describes the data used by this extension. It supplements the general FuzeVPN privacy policy, linked below. Optional diagnostic sending is separate from the data needed to sign in and use the VPN.

Notice updated on 19 October 2026

Account and VPN service

The extension exchanges authentication, account, subscription, device and proxy-session data with api.fuzevpn.com. The VPN proxy carries the traffic routed through it. These connections expose network metadata to the receiving service; this notice makes no separate promise about server logging.

Data stored in your browser

Browser storage holds your session, account details, preferences, site exclusions and device identity. Technical journals are kept locally for up to 10 minutes, even when automatic reporting is off. Diagnostic drafts and queued reports expire locally after 24 hours; cleanup runs when the extension can execute.

Public IP checks and third parties

IP display and network diagnostics contact api.ipify.org. IP display may use ipv4.icanhazip.com as a fallback. The provider receives the connection IP and ordinary request metadata: your public IP in direct mode, or the VPN exit IP when proxied. These requests omit cookies and referrers and send no account token or browsing history.

What diagnostic reports contain

Reports can include a random report identifier, timestamps, extension and browser versions, operating-system family and architecture, connection and protection states, error codes, counts, durations and check results. Full reports can include recent technical events. Their JSON excludes passwords, authentication tokens, email addresses, IP addresses, visited URLs and excluded domains. You can inspect and export a full report before sending it.

Recipient and account attribution

Reports are sent to FuzeVPN at api.fuzevpn.com/v1/diagnostics over an authenticated connection. This API connection bypasses the VPN proxy: FuzeVPN receives your direct public IP even while the VPN is active. Reports are linked to the signed-in account, not anonymous. The authentication header and IP are separate from the report JSON. A successful receipt confirms acceptance by that endpoint, not that a support agent has read the report.

Optional sending and withdrawal

Automatic sending is off by default. Enabling it permits simple error reports for that account. Manual full-report sending requires the send checkbox. Firefox also requires its optional technical-data permission. Disable automatic sending to stop it and remove pending automatic reports; cancel individual pending sends separately. Refusing diagnostic sending does not prevent VPN use.

Local deletion and downloaded files

In Diagnostics, clear technical history or delete an individual local report. Clearing history does not erase existing report copies. Cancelling or deleting locally cannot recall data already received by the service, including an upload that arrived before cancellation. Downloaded exports are separate files that you must delete yourself.

Server retention and requests

This extension does not control or verify server retention or server-side deletion. For the retention period applied by FuzeVPN and requests concerning reports already sent, consult the general privacy policy or contact FuzeVPN. No fixed server-retention period is guaranteed by this notice.

Diagnostics and support are separate

Sending a diagnostic report does not itself send a support message from the extension. To explain a problem or request deletion of a received report, use the FuzeVPN contact channel below. If you choose to share an exported file through another channel, check its contents first.
